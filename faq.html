Find the basics about delivery, orders, and getting in touch with Soko & Co.

What does delivery cost?

The demo store calculates KES 300 delivery for orders below KES 5,000, and free delivery from KES 5,000.

Can I place a real order or make a payment here?

Not yet. This is a local demo store; checkout does not process payments or create real orders.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery areas and time estimates haven’t been configured for this demo, so we can’t provide a delivery estimate yet.

What is your returns or exchange policy?

A returns and exchanges policy hasn’t been published for this demo yet.

How can I contact the shop?

The contact page has a message form, but it is not connected to email or messaging yet and will not send submissions.

Are product prices and stock live?

No. Product information, prices, and stock are demo values and have not been connected to live inventory.

This storefront is a local demo and does not accept real orders yet.
